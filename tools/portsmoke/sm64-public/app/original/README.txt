Put one supported Super Mario 64 US 1.0 ROM in this directory.
The filename is arbitrary. Z64, V64 and N64 byte order are recognized by
contents. ROM hacks and other regions are not currently supported.

First launch extracts/cooks your local data and builds the original engine.
The app's ROM copy is deleted only after the private runtime passes durable
cache verification. Keep a separate backup of your original dump elsewhere.
No ROM or generated game-data cache is included in public downloads.
